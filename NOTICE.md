# Notice

## Educational purpose

This is a **college project**. It exists to learn: how a large consumer app organises
its network surface, how a proxy normalises third-party JSON, how session auth works
when you build it yourself, and how a UI is put together without a framework.

It is coursework, not a startup. It is not monetised, not published as a service, and
not a substitute for the real app. If you are marking it, or reviewing it: that is the
lens to read it through.

## What this repository is

A **research and learning artefact**. It studies how a large consumer app organises its own network surface — how a listing page is assembled, how a menu is fetched, what parameters an endpoint needs — and rebuilds an interface over what it learned, so the behaviour can be inspected and taught.

The endpoints were discovered by probing the app's own traffic. No documentation was consulted, no SDK was wrapped, nothing was taken from a captured browser session.

## What it is not

- Not affiliated with, endorsed by, or connected to Swiggy or Bundl Technologies.
- Not a product, not a service, not an offer to buy or deliver anything.
- Not a redistribution of Swiggy's catalogue. Data is fetched live, per request, and cached in memory for two minutes. Nothing is warehoused. Orders, payments and delivery are simulated, and the UI says so.
- Not a client for anyone else's account. See [POLICY.md](POLICY.md) — that boundary is enforced in code, not just documented.
- Not authenticated against Swiggy at all. The sign-in here issues a key for *this app's* local session; no third-party credential is stored, replayed or proxied.

## Before you run it

Whether this kind of access is permitted is decided by Swiggy's terms of service, and that is your call to make, not this repository's. If you deploy or fork it:

- Keep it private, personal and low-volume. It is not built for traffic and will not behave well under it.
- Do not monetise it, do not present it as an official client, do not put it behind a domain that could be mistaken for Swiggy's.
- Do not point it at accounts you do not control.
- Stop if the service you're querying asks you to.

The polite-exit is built in: everything goes through one proxy (`server.js`) with a two-minute cache, a desktop Chrome user agent, and no retry storms.
